How RAG turns company documents into reviewed replies

Retrieval-Augmented Generation retrieves evidence before drafting. Documents supply context; they do not train the model.

1Prepare company knowledge

2Retrieve relevant evidence

3Draft, validate and review

Why the answers can be more accurate

Overlapping chunks preserve context. Scoped retrieval supplies company evidence. The tool-free drafting agent is instructed to avoid invented facts. Source checks and human approval add safeguards. A valid citation is not proof that every claim is supported.

Current setup and verification limits

Keyword search is active; hybrid embeddings are optional. Keyword search can miss paraphrases. Live AI answer quality remains unverified because API credits were exhausted. RAG improves grounding, but does not guarantee accuracy.
